Your team owns the millisecond-budget layer of the broker. Every order — and there are millions a day — passes through your pre-trade <abbr title="Risk Management System">RMS</abbr> pipeline. Your real-time <abbr title="Mark-to-Market">MTM</abbr> is what determines whether MIS positions auto-square or stay open. The four peak-margin snapshots that fire at 11:30 / 12:30 / 13:30 / 14:30 are your KPIs.

When things break, you're the one whose phone rings first. This page is your shortcut.

## What you'll find useful here

Three layers matter most:

- **Trading-day deep dives** — <abbr title="Order Management System">OMS</abbr> internals, RMS / <abbr title="Standard Portfolio Analysis of Risk">SPAN</abbr> methodology, surveillance, retail algo framework.
- **Integration <abbr title="Directed Acyclic Graph.">DAG</abbr> (trading hours)** — pre-trade pipeline, snapshot mechanics, intraday loops.
- **Margin / surveillance domains in the Compliance Blueprint** — the obligations driving your KPIs.

Everything else is context for the layer you operate at.

That's the foundation.

<Aside type="tip">
**Peak margin snapshots are the most operationally consequential events of your day.** The clearing corp captures broker margin state at 11:30 / 12:30 / 13:30 / 14:30 — those are time-locked. Your RMS must hold margin position stable across each snapshot. Pre-computing snapshot-relevant state at 11:25 / 12:25 / 13:25 / 14:25 (five minutes before each snapshot) avoids last-second compute pressure.
</Aside>
