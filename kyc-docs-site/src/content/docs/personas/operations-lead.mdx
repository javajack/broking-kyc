Your phone pings at 06:15 because the <abbr title="Standard Portfolio Analysis of Risk">SPAN</abbr> scanrange file is late. By 09:15 you've cleared three pre-open glitches. By the second peak-margin snapshot you're watching one client's <abbr title="Risk Management System">RMS</abbr> position with a small worry. By 17:00 you're approving the daily margin file. By 22:30 the ledger batch is running and you'd like to go home. Tomorrow you'll do it again, with an extra layer of weekly <abbr title="Client Funding Report.">CFR</abbr>, monthly client funding, and a quarterly running-account settlement.

This page is your shortcut.

## What you'll find useful here

You operate at the intersection of every other persona's concerns — the engineer's systems, the compliance officer's evidence, the finance officer's funds, the product manager's edge cases. You don't need depth in all of it; you need a working mental model of the whole.

Two layers matter most:

- **The Broker Process Narrative** — chronological story of the operational day, week, month, quarter, year. Your reading list.
- **The Integration <abbr title="Directed Acyclic Graph.">DAG</abbr>** — when things break, you want to know what downstream depends on what.

Then specific deep-dives for the items that keep coming up in your day.
