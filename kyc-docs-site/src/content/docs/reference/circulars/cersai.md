### CKYC/2026/08

- **date_issued:** 2026-04-22
- **issuer:** CERSAI
- **title:** "Management of Legacy Data at CKYCRR by the Reporting Entities"
- **applies_to:** all-intermediaries
- **in_force_date:** immediate
- **status:** in-force
- **impact_areas:** onboarding, re-<abbr title="Know Your Customer (process).">KYC</abbr>, kyc-modification, file-format, reporting, system-audit
- **primary_url:** https://www.ckycindia.in/ckyc/assets/doc/CommuniqueManagementOfLegacyData.pdf

Notes that CKYCRR holds approximately 112 crore digitised CKYC records and that legacy-data compliance among REs remains a substantive concern (records held in silos prior to the framework becoming operative on 01-01-2017; CKYC records generated by one RE not linked by another; identifier mismatches). Prescribes a multi-parameter parallel-search workflow (Mobile, <abbr title="Permanent Account Number">PAN</abbr>, last-4 of Aadhaar+Name+DOB+Gender, Voter ID, DL, Passport, NREGA, NPR, CKYC reference ID) before resorting to fresh CKYC upload. Recommends SFTP bulk-search for REs not yet API-integrated, with migration to real-time API "at the earliest." Re-iterates the sequential search → download → fresh-upload process to achieve CKYC compliance for legacy customers.

### CKYC/2025/18

- **date_issued:** 2025-12-12
- **issuer:** CERSAI
- **title:** "New Feature to Flag KYC Records of Deceased Customers in CKYCRR"
- **applies_to:** all-intermediaries
- **in_force_date:** 2025-12-19
- **status:** in-force
- **impact_areas:** kyc-modification, dormant, closure, transmission, nominee, file-format, reporting
- **primary_url:** https://www.ckycindia.in/ckyc/assets/doc/Communique_2025_18_Flagging_of_KYC_records_of_Deceased_customers.pdf

Introduces a new feature in CKYCRR enabling REs to flag KYC records of deceased customers via the CKYCRR Update process. On receipt of demise confirmation, the RE may flag the record as "Deceased" along with date of demise, documentary evidence and remarks in the "Other Details/Deceased Information" section of the "Update KYC – Individual" screen. Once flagged, all linked REs receive update notifications; flagged records will not be available for download but will remain retrievable through search responses with an 'X' prefix preceding the KYC identifier. Bulk Update file structure v1.3 is referenced. Effective December 19, 2025, 8 pm.

- **title:** "KYC Search through CKYC API and Bulk Search for Account Opening"

- **in_force_date:** immediate
- **status:** in-force

- **primary_url:** https://www.ckycindia.in/ckyc/assets/doc/Communique_Search_process_through_APIs_and_SFTP_Dec2025.pdf

Operational guidance addressing complaints that REs were unable to retrieve CKYC records via search despite confirmed-match results at upload. Encourages multi-parameter CKYC search at the front end (single click triggering multiple API requests, with results consolidated client-side) and bulk search via SFTP for REs not yet integrated with APIs. Document carries no formal communique number on the header but is dated 03 December 2025.

### CKYC/2025/10

- **applies_to:** all-intermediaries
- **in_force_date:** immediate
- **status:** in-force

- **primary_url:** https://www.ckycindia.in/ckyc/assets/doc/Communique_2025_10-Advisory_on_Usage_of_CKYC_Services_via_Third-Party_Vendors.pdf

Following complaints of unauthorised downloads and multiple update transactions traced to third-party-vendor technical glitches, CERSAI cautions that third-party vendors are technical service providers engaged by an RE "at their own risk and responsibility" and that no operational lapse will absolve the RE of its responsibility. REs availing third-party services are required to (1) exercise enhanced due-diligence and oversight in line with regulatory outsourcing guidelines, (2) review internal processes and access controls related to CKYC data, and (3) ensure compliance with information-security and data-access guidelines.
