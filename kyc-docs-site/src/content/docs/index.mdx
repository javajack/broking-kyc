Indian stock broking operations are an interlocking system: a 9-screen onboarding pipeline that culminates in 8 parallel regulatory batch uploads; a daily trading rhythm from 06:00 <abbr title="Beginning Of Day">BOD</abbr> scripts through to overnight settlement; quarterly running-account settlements; half-yearly compliance certificates; an annual audit calendar that pulls together every previous cycle; a regulatory base of 884 circulars; a vendor stack of 233 named products; six post-onboarding lifecycle scenarios that an active client account will hit. This site documents all of it, with verifiable citations and persona-curated reading paths for whoever shows up.
