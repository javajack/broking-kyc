This site documents all of it, with verifiable citations and persona-curated reading paths for whoever shows up.

:::note[Why so many vendors?]
Indian securities regulation requires identity verification through multiple independent sources -- PAN through NSDL/Protean, KYC status through KRA agencies, central identity through CKYC (CERSAI), and document verification through DigiLocker. No single vendor covers all of these. The vendor strategy section explains how we stitch them together.
:::
