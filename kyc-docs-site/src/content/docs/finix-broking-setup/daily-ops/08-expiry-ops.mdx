> **Why this page is structured this way:** Expiry is predictable, which makes almost every expiry problem preventable. The page is therefore a timeline that starts well before expiry week rather than on expiry day, because the decisions that matter — tell the client, review the position, plan the funding — have to be made while there is still time to act on them. Expiry day itself is mostly execution and monitoring; the work is earlier.

## TL;DR

- **Two concentration days a week now, not one.** <abbr title="National Stock Exchange of India">NSE</abbr>'s weekly index expiry is **Tuesday** and <abbr title="BSE Limited (formerly Bombay Stock Exchange)">BSE</abbr>'s is **Thursday**, following the standardisation effective 1 September 2025.
- **Delivery margins build across the last sessions of expiry week**, not on expiry day. The step change arrives before most clients are thinking about expiry.
- **Client communication starts well before expiry week.** A client who discovers a physical-settlement obligation on expiry day has been failed by the communication, not by the market.
- **A failed square-off is not an exit.** If illiquidity or a price band blocks the closing trade, the residual quantity stays in the delivery queue and becomes an obligation.
- **Commodity tender days have a 19:30 eClear deadline** and a margin ladder that starts three trading days before expiry.
- **Capacity concentrates.** Order rates, modification rates and square-off activity all peak in the final hour, and the firm's own risk engine is one of the largest order generators.
- **The day after expiry is a reconciliation day**: delivery obligations, assignments, residual positions and corrected contract notes.

## 1. Framework

- **Expiry standardisation** — one weekly benchmark index contract per exchange; **NSE Tuesday, BSE Thursday**, effective 1 September 2025 following <abbr title="Securities and Exchange Board of India">SEBI</abbr>'s circular of 26 May 2025. `[not yet in index]`
- **Physical settlement of single-stock derivatives** — mandated by SEBI/<abbr title="Head Office (SEBI circular ID prefix)">HO</abbr>/<abbr title="Market Regulation Department (SEBI)">MRD</abbr>/DOPI/CIR/P/2018/161 (31 December 2018), phased across the 2019 expiries. `[not yet in index]`
- **Delivery margin staging** — staged across the last sessions of expiry week per <abbr title="NSE Clearing Limited (formerly National Securities Clearing Corporation Limited)">NCL</abbr>/CMPT/43262 (17 January 2020). `[not yet in index]`
- **[NCL/CMPT/55330](/broking-kyc/reference/circulars/clearing-corps/#nclcmpt55330)** (20 January 2023) as updated by **[NCL/CMPT/56167](/broking-kyc/reference/circulars/clearing-corps/#nclcmpt56167)** (28 March 2023) — netting of derivatives delivery obligations with cash-segment obligations for the same clearing member, trading member and unique client code.
- **Expiry withdraws the cross-margin spread benefit on the expiring leg** — index contracts from 1 February 2025 under [SEBI/HO/MRD/TPD/P/CIR/2024/132](/broking-kyc/reference/circulars/sebi-other/#sebihomrdtpdpcir2024132); single stocks per the February 2026 instruction effective three months from issue.
- **Commodity staggered delivery** — tender period over the last three trading days including expiry, with an **8.34 percent incremental margin**; delivery intention until **19:30** through <abbr title="Multi Commodity Exchange Clearing Corporation Limited">MCXCCL</abbr> eClear; delivery period margin at the higher of 3 percent plus five-day 99 percent value-at-risk of spot volatility, or 25 percent.
- Depth: [expiry day operations](/broking-kyc/trading-risk/expiry-day-operations/), [exercise, assignment and physical settlement](/broking-kyc/trading-risk/exercise-assignment-physical/).

## 2. The timeline

| When | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Monthly, in advance** | Expiry calendar published internally; capacity and staffing planned | Operations |
| **Several sessions before expiry week** | **Client communication**: which positions expire, what physical settlement means, what the margin ladder will do | Support and risk |
| **Several sessions before** | Commodity clients told about the tender period and the delivery-period margin | Support and risk |
| Start of expiry week | Position review: who holds what, who will have a delivery obligation | Risk |
| Start of expiry week | **Forward funding view** for the delivery-margin ladder | Treasury |
| **Across expiry week** | **Delivery margins stage upward**; margin calls follow | Risk |
| Expiry week | Cross-margin spread benefit withdrawn on expiring legs | Risk |
| Expiry week | Clients contacted about positions heading into delivery | Support |
| **Expiry day, pre-open** | Final position review; square-off decision points identified | Risk |
| Expiry day, session | Elevated monitoring; capacity watched | All |
| **Expiry day, final hour** | Concentration peak; square-off execution; residual tracking | Risk and dealing |
| Expiry day, close | Residual positions recorded; delivery obligations known | Risk and operations |
| **Expiry day, evening** | Delivery obligations processed; netting applied | Operations |
| **Day after** | Reconciliation: assignments, deliveries, residuals, corrections | Operations |

<Aside type="caution" title="The client who is surprised on expiry day was failed a week earlier">
Physical settlement, the delivery-margin ladder and the commodity tender period are all published, predictable and computable from the client's own position days in advance. A client who learns on expiry day that their option position creates a share-delivery obligation, or that their commodity position now requires a margin an order of magnitude larger, has not been caught out by the market — they have been failed by a communication that should have gone several sessions earlier. Treat the pre-expiry communication as the primary control, with square-off as the fallback rather than the plan.
</Aside>

## 3. Position review

| Review | When | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Positions expiring this week** | Start of expiry week | A list, by client, by instrument |
| **Which will create a delivery obligation** | Start of expiry week | Direction matters: long futures and exercised long calls receive; short futures, assigned short calls and exercised long puts deliver |
| Clients without the capacity to take or make delivery | Start of expiry week | Early contact |
| Clients without an active demat account | Start of expiry week | **Cannot settle a delivery**; urgent |
| Positions in illiquid contracts | Mid-week | **Square-off may fail**; plan earlier |
| Positions in instruments under a restriction | Mid-week | A band, halt or call auction can block the exit |
| Commodity positions heading into the tender period | **Before the tender period starts** | The margin ladder and the tender deadline |
| Cross-margin positions with an expiring leg | Before the benefit is withdrawn | The requirement will rise |

## 4. Square-off decision points

| Point | Consideration |
|---|---|
| Early in expiry week | Liquidity is best; the client has the most options |
| Mid-week | The delivery ladder is building; margin pressure begins |
| **Expiry day, early** | Still liquid; the firm has time to act if an exit fails |
| **Expiry day, final hour** | **Liquidity thins, prices move, and a failed exit becomes an obligation** |
| After close | Too late; the position settles |

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Decide early | The firm's disclosed policy should state what it does with positions heading into delivery |
| **A failed square-off is not an exit** | The residual stays in the delivery queue; escalate rather than retry |
| Restrictions apply | A banned underlying still permits reduction; a halted or band-locked instrument does not |
| Lot size | Derivatives cannot be partially exited below one lot; a structural residual |
| Communication | Before acting, where time allows; see [square-off engine](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/rms/06-squareoff-engine/) |

## 5. Capacity on expiry day

| Pressure | Source |
|---|---|
| Order rate | Client activity concentrated in the final hour |
| Modification and cancellation rate | Repositioning |
| **Square-off generation** | **The firm's own engine, concurrent with the volatility that triggered it** |
| Market data | Tick rates multiply |
| Risk computation | Every position revalued repeatedly |
| Client logins and screens | Concentration |
| Support contact | Concentration |
| **Two days a week** | NSE Tuesday and BSE Thursday |

| Preparation | Detail |
|---|---|
| Load-tested at the measured peak **including the square-off burst** | See [infrastructure and connectivity](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/setup/08-infrastructure-connectivity/#31-capacity-sizing) |
| Staffing uplift on both concentration days | Dealing, risk, support, technology |
| No changes deployed on expiry days | A standing rule |
| Monitoring attention raised | Latency, rejection rate, queue depths |
| Escalation pre-staged | Named people available |

## 6. Physical settlement handling

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Direction determined | Per leg, not per profit: long future and exercised long call receive; short future, assigned short call and exercised long put deliver; out-of-the-money options produce nothing |
| Obligation computed | Per client |
| **Netting** | Derivatives delivery obligations net against cash-segment obligations for the same clearing member, trading member and unique client code; **securities tax and stamp duty remain segment-wise** |
| Delivery instructions | Through the depository, by the deadline |
| Shortfall | The short-delivery path; see [settlement day operations](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/daily-ops/06-settlement-day-ops/#6-short-delivery) |
| Unpaid obligations | <abbr title="Client Unpaid Securities Pledgee Account.">CUSPA</abbr> applies at pay-out |
| Client communication | What they must deliver or will receive, and by when |
| Corporate actions | Can accelerate an expiry and change the obligation |

## 7. Commodity tender days

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Window | The last three trading days including expiry, **excluding special sessions such as Muhurat** |
| Margin | **8.34 percent incremental** across those days, on top of the delivery period margin |
| **Deadline** | **19:30** for delivery intention through MCXCCL eClear |
| Staffing | **A named, certified, present, authorised person** on every tender day |
| Seller early pay-in | Exempts all margins except mark-to-market, with documentary evidence |
| Clearing route | Through the clearing member at launch; their timetable governs |
| Escalation | Before 19:30, not at it |

## 8. The day after

| # | Activity | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Expiry processing verified: settled, assigned, delivered | Operations |
| 2 | **Assignment outcomes reconciled** against client positions | Operations and risk |
| 3 | Delivery obligations reconciled, including netting | Operations |
| 4 | Residual positions from failed square-offs reconciled | Risk |
| 5 | Short deliveries identified and the auction path started | Operations |
| 6 | Contract notes for expiry-day activity verified | Operations |
| 7 | Margin released on expired positions | Risk |
| 8 | Client queries handled — assignments are the main theme | Support |
| 9 | **Review**: what surprised clients, what surprised the firm | All |
| 10 | Next expiry's communication plan adjusted | Support and risk |

## 10. Exception paths

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Square-off fails on illiquidity | Escalate; the residual becomes a delivery obligation; inform the client |
| Instrument band-locked or halted during the final hour | Cannot exit; escalate; manage the resulting obligation |
| Client without a demat account holding a delivery-bound position | Urgent: resolve or close the position; a client who cannot settle is a short delivery |
| Corporate action accelerates an expiry | **Prominent, immediate communication**; review delivery consequences |
| Capacity strain in the final hour | Expected; the plan should cover it; rate-limit by client if needed, never globally |
| Commodity tender deadline approaching without cover | Escalate before 19:30 |
| Assignment outcome disputed | Produce the position, the assignment and the direction rules |
| Short delivery after expiry | The auction path; costs allocated per the disclosed policy |
| Cross-margin benefit withdrawal causes a breach | Predictable; the forward view should have flagged it |
| Both exchanges' expiries in the same week with a holiday | Concentration shifts; re-plan staffing |

## Practical notes

- **Communicate several sessions before expiry week, not during it.** It is the primary control and it is cheap.
- **Review positions against demat status early.** A client who cannot settle is a short delivery with a known cost.
- **Treat a failed square-off as an escalation, not a retry.** The residual is an obligation.
- **Do not deploy on expiry days.** It should be a standing rule, not a judgement call.
- **Load-test with the square-off burst included.** The firm's own engine is a major order generator at exactly the wrong moment.
- **Staff both concentration days.** Tuesday and Thursday now both carry weight.
- **Review after every expiry.** The surprises repeat unless the communication plan changes.
