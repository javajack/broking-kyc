> **Why this page is structured this way:** The pre-open is short, high-attention and has phase-specific rules, so the page is organised by phase with the monitoring and the client-facing consequence of each. It also carries the one piece of client education that produces the most support contact in the whole trading day — why an order filled at a price the client did not specify — because handling that well at 09:12 is worth more than handling it well in a help article.

## TL;DR

- **The after-market queue is released into the pre-open order-entry window**, which makes its verification the first client-visible event of the day. Verify with counts; do not assume.
- **Three phases with different rules**: order entry 09:00 to 09:08, matching and trade confirmation 09:08 to 09:12, buffer 09:12 to 09:15. Order entry and modification behave differently in each.
- **Trades execute at a discovered price**, not at the order's limit. This is the single largest source of "why did my order fill there" contact, and it arrives in a two-minute window.
- **Monitoring in the pre-open is about readiness, not volume.** Session state, order acceptance, the release count, and whether risk is evaluating.
- **The pre-open is where a bad begin-of-day shows up.** A limit that resolves wrongly, a master that is stale in one instrument, a session that is up but not accepting — all surface here rather than at 09:15.
- **Commodity and currency segments open on their own schedules**, so "pre-open" is not a single firm-wide state.
- **Handover into continuous trading is explicit**, with open items named.

## 1. Framework

- **Pre-open phases** — order entry 09:00 to 09:08, order matching and trade confirmation 09:08 to 09:12, buffer 09:12 to 09:15, with continuous trading from 09:15. See [pre-open and closing auction](/broking-kyc/deep-dives/foundational/pre-open-closing-auction/).
- **After-market orders** placed between the previous close and the pre-open are **released into the pre-open at 09:00**, entering the order-entry queue.
- **Call-auction variants** for initial public offerings and relisted scrips, including indicative quantity and price disclosure, per <abbr title="Securities and Exchange Board of India">SEBI</abbr>/<abbr title="Head Office (SEBI circular ID prefix)">HO</abbr>/<abbr title="Market Regulation Department (SEBI)">MRD</abbr>/MRD-PoD-3/P/CIR/2024/85 (20 June 2024) and **<abbr title="National Stock Exchange of India">NSE</abbr>/CMTR/63594** (17 September 2024). `[not yet in index]`
- **Session state** is driven by exchange market-state messages; see [sessions and calendars](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/exchanges/06-sessions-calendars/).

## 2. By phase

### 09:00 — release and order entry opens

| Step | Owner | Assertion |
|---|---|---|
| **After-market queue released** | Technology | **Count released matches count queued**; rejections identified and classified |
| Session state confirmed as pre-open entry | Technology | Per exchange and segment |
| Order acceptance verified | Dealing | A test or first live order accepted and acknowledged |
| Risk evaluating | Risk | Decisions being made; latency within target |
| Client channels up | Technology | Web, mobile, terminal, API |
| Rejection monitoring begins | Dealing and risk | Rate and code distribution |

| Watch for | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Release count mismatch | Orders lost or duplicated; **incident** |
| High rejection rate on released orders | Usually a master, band or limit problem from begin-of-day |
| Orders rejected for limits | A collateral or ledger load issue surfacing |
| No orders flowing | A channel or session problem, not a quiet market at 09:00 |

### 09:00 to 09:08 — order entry

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| Client order entry | Active; modification and cancellation permitted |
| Dealer activity | Active |
| Monitoring | Acceptance, rejection codes, latency, session stability |
| Risk | Evaluating normally; limits in force |
| Market data | Indicative prices where published |
| Client questions | Mostly about the after-market release |

### 09:08 to 09:12 — matching and trade confirmation

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Order entry closed** | New orders, modifications and cancellations not accepted |
| Matching | The exchange discovers the opening price and executes at it |
| **Trade confirmations arrive** | Positions, margins and client views update |
| Risk | Post-trade positions recomputed; utilisation changes |
| **Client contact spikes** | "Why did my order fill at a different price" |
| Monitoring | Trade confirmation receipt; position and margin propagation |

<Aside type="caution" title="The two-minute explanation window">
Between 09:08 and 09:12 a large number of clients see fills at prices they did not specify, all at once, because the opening price is discovered rather than quoted. If the explanation is in a help article, they contact support. If it is in the fill notification and on the order screen — "executed at the pre-open discovered price of ₹X" — most of them do not. This is the highest-leverage piece of client education in the trading day, and it costs one line of interface copy.
</Aside>

### 09:12 to 09:15 — buffer

| Activity | Detail |
|---|---|
| No new entry | Transition period |
| Reconciliation | Pre-open trades reconciled; counts checked |
| Position and margin verification | Post-auction positions confirmed before continuous trading |
| **Readiness check for continuous** | Session state, risk, channels |
| Handover | Pre-open monitoring to session monitoring |

### 09:15 — continuous trading

| Step | Owner |
|---|---|
| Session state confirmed as open | Technology |
| First continuous orders monitored | Dealing |
| Latency and rejection baselines established | Technology |
| Handover complete, with open items named | All |

## 3. Monitoring during pre-open

| Signal | Why it matters here |
|---|---|
| **After-market release count** | The first thing that can go wrong, and clients notice |
| Session state per exchange and segment | Three exchanges, one of them with per-segment sessions |
| Order acceptance rate | A begin-of-day problem surfaces as rejections |
| **Rejection code distribution** | Tells you which begin-of-day gate was wrong |
| Risk decision latency | Capacity warm-up |
| Limits resolving | Clients hitting the global default means a configuration gap |
| Market data arrival | Staleness detection starts as feeds begin |
| Channel availability | Login volume concentrates at the open |
| Client contact volume | A spike before 09:08 usually means a release problem |

## 4. Multi-segment reality

| Segment | Pre-open behaviour |
|---|---|
| NSE cash | Full pre-open as described |
| NSE derivatives | Per the segment's rules |
| <abbr title="BSE Limited (formerly Bombay Stock Exchange)">BSE</abbr> segments | **Per instance**, with independent state |
| Currency derivatives | Own schedule |
| **<abbr title="Multi Commodity Exchange of India">MCX</abbr>** | Opens at 09:00 on its own schedule, with no equity-style pre-open |

"Pre-open" is therefore not a firm-wide state. The operations view must be per exchange and per segment, and client messaging must name the segment.

## 5. Common pre-open incidents

| Incident | First response |
|---|---|
| After-market release fails or is partial | **Named incident.** Quantify, inform clients, decide whether to re-release or cancel; see [incident playbooks](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/daily-ops/07-incident-playbooks/) |
| Released orders rejected en masse | Check the begin-of-day gate that produced the rejection code |
| Session up but orders not accepted | Technology escalation; verify the state message |
| Risk not evaluating | **No order entry**; escalate immediately; there is no bypass |
| Limits wrong across many clients | The collateral or ledger load; this should have been caught at G5 |
| Market data absent | Block price-dependent decisions; escalate |
| A channel down | Segment- and channel-specific client message |
| Exchange delays the pre-open | Follow the state message; inform clients |

## 6. Configuration reference

| Key | Finix default | Class | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| `finix.ops.preopen.amo_release_verify` | **Count-verified** | Discretionary, strongly recommended | Silent non-release |
| `finix.ops.preopen.amo_rejection_classification` | By reason | Discretionary | Begin-of-day problems undiagnosed |
| `finix.ops.preopen.phase_state_source` | Exchange market-state messages | Exchange-set | Entry in the wrong phase |
| `finix.ops.preopen.discovered_price_messaging` | In the fill notification and on the order screen | Discretionary, strongly recommended | Avoidable support volume |
| `finix.ops.preopen.per_segment_view` | True | Discretionary, strongly recommended | "The market is down" messaging |
| `finix.ops.preopen.handover_written` | True | Discretionary | Open items lost at 09:15 |

## 7. Exception paths

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Release count does not match the queue | Incident; quantify; inform clients; decide re-release or cancel |
| Orders released into a closed session | Calendar-awareness failure; incident |
| Mass rejections on released orders | Trace the rejection code to the begin-of-day gate; fix before continuous trading if possible |
| Exchange extends or delays the pre-open | Follow the state message; inform clients; do not act on the clock |
| Client disputes a pre-open fill price | Explain the discovery mechanism; the order and trade records support it |
| Risk latency high at 09:00 | Capacity warm-up; if it persists past the open, treat as an incident |
| One BSE instance not in pre-open | That segment only; message precisely |
| Position or margin not propagated after matching | Stop increasing exposure for affected clients; reconcile |

## Practical notes

- **Verify the release with counts and classify the rejections.** The rejection codes tell you which gate was wrong.
- **Put the discovered-price explanation in the fill notification.** It is the highest-leverage copy in the product.
- **Keep the operations view per segment.** Three exchanges, and BSE has per-segment sessions.
- **Treat "risk not evaluating" as a stop, not a degradation.** There is no bypass, and the pre-open is the moment to find out.
- **Write the handover.** Open items at 09:15 are the ones that become incidents at 11:00.
- **Watch client contact volume before 09:08.** A spike there is almost always the release.
