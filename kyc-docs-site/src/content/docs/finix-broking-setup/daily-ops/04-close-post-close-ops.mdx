> **Why this page is structured this way:** The close is a short window in which the day's loose ends either get tied or get carried into the record pipeline, where they are much more expensive. The page is therefore a checklist of what must be resolved before end-of-day processing begins — unknown orders, unexplained differences, pending freezes — followed by the handover, and a reminder that for a member of <abbr title="Multi Commodity Exchange of India">MCX</abbr> the day is not over.

## TL;DR

- **The closing session is a separate mechanism**, running 15:30 to 15:40 and determining the closing price by its own rules.
- **Day orders are cancelled by the exchange at end of session.** Process the bulk state transition and release the margin blocks.
- **Orders in `UNKNOWN` must be resolved before the session ends.** An unknown order carried into end-of-day becomes a reconciliation difference and potentially a settlement problem.
- **Pending freezes must be resolved**, not left holding margin overnight.
- **Final intraday reconciliation happens here**, not in the end-of-day pipeline — order and trade counts against the exchange, and positions against the firm's book.
- **The handover into end-of-day is written**, with open items named and owned.
- **For a member of MCX, the day is not over.** The commodity session runs to 23:30 while the equity pipeline processes, and the commodity close is a separate, later checklist.

## 1. The closing session

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Window | 15:30 to 15:40 |
| Purpose | Closing price determination |
| Mechanism | Per the exchange's closing session rules; distinct from continuous trading |
| Order behaviour | Per the session's own rules |
| Monitoring | Session state, acceptance, trade confirmations |
| Client questions | Closing prices and the mechanism; same education problem as the pre-open, at lower volume |

## 2. Post-close checklist

| # | Step | Owner | Assertion | If not met |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Session state confirmed as post-close, per exchange and segment | Technology | State message received | Investigate; do not infer from the clock |
| 2 | **Day orders cancelled by the exchange** | Operations | Bulk transition processed; counts reconcile | Reconcile against the exchange |
| 3 | **Margin blocks released** for cancelled orders | Risk | Utilisation reflects the release | Investigate; clients are holding blocks they should not |
| 4 | **Orders in `UNKNOWN` resolved** | Operations and technology | **Zero remaining** | **Escalate**; reconcile with the exchange before end-of-day |
| 5 | **Pending freezes resolved** | Dealing | None left holding margin | Escalate to the exchange |
| 6 | **Final intraday reconciliation** — order and trade counts, values, positions | Operations | Matches the exchange | **Investigate before end-of-day processing** |
| 7 | Open client warnings and grace periods reviewed | Risk | Each has a disposition for tomorrow | Carry with an owner |
| 8 | Unresolved square-offs and residual positions recorded | Risk | Known and owned | Escalate |
| 9 | Surveillance alerts from the session triaged | Compliance | Within the service level | Carry with ageing visible |
| 10 | Pay-in attribution queue cleared | Treasury | Clients not left waiting overnight | Work it |
| 11 | Pay-out queue status reviewed | Treasury | Terminal states known | Investigate pending items |
| 12 | Client contact themes reviewed | Support | Any theme the board did not surface | Investigate the gap |
| 13 | **Written handover into end-of-day** | All | Signed | — |

<Aside type="caution" title="Resolve unknown orders here, not in the pipeline">
An order in the `UNKNOWN` state is one the firm sent and for which it never received an outcome. Carried into end-of-day it becomes a trade-file reconciliation difference, which blocks contract notes, which puts a regulatory deadline at risk. Resolving it at 15:45 is a conversation with the exchange; resolving it at 19:00 is the same conversation with a deadline running. Make zero unknown orders an explicit post-close assertion.
</Aside>

## 3. What must not be carried forward

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| **Orders in `UNKNOWN`** | Become reconciliation differences and settlement problems |
| **Unexplained order or trade count differences** | Block contract notes |
| **Pending freezes** | Hold client margin overnight with no resolution |
| Unreleased margin blocks | Clients are constrained for no reason |
| Unattributed pay-ins | Clients waiting for capacity overnight |
| Position differences against the exchange | A risk difference, not an administrative one |
| Open severity-one incidents | Should have escalated already |

## 4. What is legitimately carried

| Item | With |
|---|---|
| Client warnings and grace periods | A named owner and a tomorrow disposition |
| Residual positions that could not be squared off | An escalation record and a plan |
| Surveillance alerts within the service level | Visible ageing |
| Reconciliation exceptions within their ageing tolerance | Owner and age |
| Commodity positions and tender-day items | The evening shift |
| Resting broker-held intent — Good Till Triggered, Stock SIP, after-market | Reviewed against any restriction changes |

## 5. The handover into end-of-day

| Content | Detail |
|---|---|
| Gate status from the morning | Any exceptions accepted at the open |
| Incidents during the session | Open and closed |
| Reconciliation status | Counts matched, or differences and their investigation |
| Clients under warning | With amounts and dispositions |
| Residual positions | With owners |
| Treasury position | What was funded, what is outstanding |
| Pay-in and pay-out queue status | Items carried |
| **Anything that could delay contract notes** | The deadline runs from execution |
| Commodity status | Positions, tender-day items, evening cover |
| Signed | By both shifts |

## 6. The commodity reality

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| MCX still trading | To **23:30**, or **23:55** in United States daylight saving |
| Concurrency | The equity end-of-day pipeline runs alongside |
| Separation | **Two operating modes in the same building**; separate handovers and separate checklists |
| Tender days | The **19:30** eClear deadline falls during this period |
| Commodity close | A **separate, later checklist**; see [<abbr title="End Of Day">EOD</abbr> runbook](/broking-kyc/finix-broking-setup/daily-ops/05-eod-runbook/#8-the-commodity-close) |
| Risk cover | Must continue through the evening session |
| Treasury | Evening cover with banking channels closed |

## 8. Exception paths

| Situation | Response |
|---|---|
| Orders remain in `UNKNOWN` | Escalate to the exchange; **do not begin end-of-day processing on an unresolved book** |
| Order or trade counts differ from the exchange | Investigate now; the exchange governs |
| Position difference against the exchange | Stop increasing exposure for affected clients tomorrow until resolved |
| Freeze unresolved at close | Escalate to the exchange; decide on the margin block |
| Day-order cancellation not reflected | Reconcile against the exchange |
| A residual position could not be squared off | Escalate; decide on carrying the exposure; inform the client the same day |
| Pay-in unattributed at close | Work it; a client waiting overnight for their own money is a service failure |
| Handover not completed | The receiving shift should refuse to accept an unwritten handover |
| Commodity session issue during equity end-of-day | Two incidents, two owners; do not serialise them |

## Practical notes

- **Make zero unknown orders a hard post-close assertion.** It is the cheapest protection for the contract-note deadline.
- **Reconcile counts before the pipeline starts, not inside it.** Differences found later collide with deadlines.
- **Release margin blocks and verify it.** Clients notice constraints they cannot explain.
- **Write and sign the handover.** The items that get lost are precisely the ones that matter.
- **Keep the commodity evening on its own checklist.** It runs later, with fewer people, in parallel with a heavy pipeline.
- **Clear the pay-in queue before you go home.** Overnight waiting on a confirmed payment is a service failure with no upside.
