> **Why this page exists:** The risk plane holds the firm's most consequential configuration — the values that decide who may trade, how much, and when their positions are closed. Scattered across eight pages they are undefendable; collected here they are reviewable. The classification and mid-session columns carry most of the weight: a discretionary value needs an approved policy, and a value that may not change mid-session needs a deployment process that enforces that.

## TL;DR

- **The risk plane's discretionary values are the firm's risk appetite expressed as numbers**, and every one of them must trace to an approved, disclosed risk management policy.
- **Three values must be identical in configuration and in the client-facing policy**: the square-off warning threshold, the action threshold, and the grace period. A mismatch is the first thing an arbitrator finds.
- **Fourteen values must never change mid-session**, because each can turn a compliant client into a non-compliant one instantly and across a whole group.
- **The permissive defaults are the dangerous ones.** Timeout behaviour, staleness behaviour, collateral recognition on a missing list, and capacity on an unconfirmed receipt all have a safe setting that conflicts with the instinct to keep things flowing.
- **Every input has a freshness contract, and the contract is configuration.** The staleness threshold and the behaviour on breach are as much a control as the limit itself.
- **Configuration is point-in-time answerable and retained on the eight-year floor.** "What was this client's square-off threshold on 14 August" is a question that gets asked.

## 9. The must-not-change-mid-session set

| Key | Why |
|---|---|
| `finix.rms.squareoff.warn_pct.*` | Removing notice from clients already in the warning band |
| `finix.rms.squareoff.trigger_pct.*` | Instant mass square-off |
| `finix.rms.squareoff.grace_minutes.*` | Shortening a grace period already running |
| `finix.rms.squareoff.selection_rule` | Inconsistent treatment within one event |
| `finix.rms.limits.exposure_multiplier.*` downward | Recomputes every utilisation at once |
| `finix.rms.limits.global_default` | The resolution terminator |
| `finix.rms.limits.resolution_order` | Changes which limit applies, unpredictably |
| `finix.rms.margin.basis.*` | Changes the requirement for every client |
| `finix.rms.collateral.recognise_on` | Changes recognised capacity instantly |
| `finix.rms.collateral.cash_equiv_min_pct_cm` | Regulatory |
| `finix.rms.input.*_assert_*` | The gates themselves |
| `finix.rms.decision.timeout_action` | The core safety default |
| `finix.rms.report.basis.*` | Reporting consistency within a day |
| `finix.rms.penalty.client_passthrough_allowed` | Changes client charging basis mid-cycle |

<Aside type="caution" title="The policy-mirror set">
`squareoff.warn_pct`, `squareoff.trigger_pct`, `squareoff.grace_minutes`, `squareoff.selection_rule`, `squareoff.intraday_time`, `limits.exposure_multiplier` and `collateral.client_cash_policy` all appear in the client-facing risk management policy. Changing any of them without updating the policy means the firm is operating outside its disclosed mandate. Bind them procedurally: the change request form for these keys requires the policy version that will accompany the change, and the change does not deploy without it.
</Aside>

## 10. Review cadence

| Set | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Input freshness thresholds and actions | Quarterly, and after any incident | Risk and technology |
| Margin basis and buffers | Quarterly, and on any circular | Risk committee |
| Limits and multipliers | Quarterly; group templates monthly against overrides | Risk committee |
| Collateral recognition and caps | Quarterly, and on clearing corporation changes | Risk |
| Square-off thresholds and method | **With every policy revision**, and at least annually | Risk committee and board |
| Surveillance rules and thresholds | Monthly for volume, quarterly for coverage | Compliance |
| Reporting parameters | On every circular | Operations and compliance |
| The must-not-change-mid-session list | Annually, and whenever a key is added | Risk and technology |
