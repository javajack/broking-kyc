**Research cutoff: 15 October 2026 (India).** Scope: greenfield NSE + BSE + MCX broker; equity cash/F&O, currency and commodities; DP, institutional, MTF, advisory/research and distribution obligations identified when conditional.

| Reading rule | Meaning |

| Evidence classification | A row’s evidence tag applies to **every factual cell in that row**, unless a cell carries a different tag. `[verified]` = inspected primary authority document/page; `[likely]` = at least two named independent secondary publishers; `[uncertain]` = one secondary/reproduced source or incomplete primary extraction; `[unknown]` = not established. Links are evidence, not an assertion that an older webpage is the latest law. |
| Broker action | “Do” cells are implementation interpretations of the cited requirements, not quotations or additional regulatory mandates. Conditional activities apply only if the broker undertakes them. |
